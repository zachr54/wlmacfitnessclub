Waiver & weight-room etiquette

Lifting carries risk. You confirm you are medically clear to train and that WLMAC, its staff and its execs are not liable for injury. You will warm up, use collars, and ask for a spotter on max attempts. You will re-rack every plate, wipe the bench, and keep chalk in the bowl. You will not film anyone without asking. Every skill level is welcome in this room — no coaching anyone who did not ask for it, no comments on anyone’s body or numbers.
